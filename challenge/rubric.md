# Public Rubric

We do not expect one official answer. We are looking for judgment under ambiguity.

## Scope Judgment

Did you choose a small, useful, finishable scope?

## Domain And Intent Framing

Did you make the problem understandable? Is it clear who the artifact is for and why it matters?

## Data Judgment

Did you choose, collect, clean, or inspect data thoughtfully? Did you notice missing values, duplicated rows, inconsistent labels, suspicious spikes, weak sources, or caveats?

## Engineering Fundamentals

Does the artifact run? Is the code or structure understandable? Does it handle messiness reasonably?

## Product Thinking

Is the output useful to a teammate or user? Did you optimize for one clear use case?

## Communication

Can someone quickly understand what you built, what assumptions you made, and what you would do next?

## AI Collaboration

If you used AI, did you use it intentionally and critically? Did you verify important parts yourself?

## What We Will Not Reward More

- Bigger scope for its own sake.
- More complex models without a clear reason.
- Paid infrastructure.
- Long reports.
- Surface polish that hides unclear thinking.
